How accounts work on this site

This is a classroom project made only with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so there is no server. Your account, posts and meet-ups are saved in this browser only, and other people cannot see them.

Your password is never saved as text, only as a scrambled hash. Even so, do not reuse a real password here.

The admins are the project group: Lin Thuriya Pho Hlaing, Zayar Win Htay and Pyay Lin Thant. Group members, register with your school email and your account becomes an admin.

Your browser is blocking storage for this site, so you cannot log in or register. Allow site data in your browser settings and reload the page.
