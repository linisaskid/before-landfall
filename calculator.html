Why hours matter

When Nargis was photographed on 2 May 2008, it was only hours from the delta. A few hours is the difference between leaving and being caught in the surge.

Three steps, one simple equation

Time in hours = distance in kilometres ÷ forward speed in kilometres per hour.

  1. 01

    Find the distance

    Use the estimated distance from the storm centre to the coast in the direction it is travelling.

  2. 02

    Find forward speed

    Use the storm’s movement speed from a forecast. Wind speed describes something different.

  3. 03

    Divide, then prepare

    For example, 600 km ÷ 30 km/h = 20 hours. The site’s three time bands are classroom examples.

The 6-hour and 24-hour bands are not official warning levels. See how to use warning information.
