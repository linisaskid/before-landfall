        Community rules

        Everyone who registers agrees to these. Admins may remove posts that break them.

        1. Be kind. Many families in the Delta lost people in past storms. Write about them with care.
        2. Say where facts come from. Name the book, website or person, so others can check.
        3. Official warnings come first. This board is for learning. In a real storm, follow the Department of Meteorology and Hydrology and local leaders.
        4. Keep personal details private. Do not post phone numbers, addresses or other people’s photos.
