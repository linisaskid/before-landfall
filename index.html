In 2008, time ran out

Cyclone Nargis crossed the Ayeyarwady Delta on 2–3 May 2008.18 Forecasts reached Myanmar days before landfall, but many people did not act on the warnings, and the regional alerts did not include a storm-surge warning. Over 80% of deaths were later linked to the surge.19 See the history of Myanmar’s cyclones
