Make the plan before you need it.

A few decisions made together can make a difficult moment clearer: where to go, how to get there and who needs extra help.

Build your household plan ↓Check official warning sources

What a plan protects you from

In parts of the delta, 90–95% of buildings were destroyed.11 A plan means you are already somewhere safer when that happens.

Three things to agree together

Keep the plan practical. Make sure each person knows their part and where to find the supplies.

  1. 01

    Pack a kit

    Keep drinking water, ready-to-eat food, medicines, a torch, batteries and first-aid supplies together.

  2. 02

    Choose a safe place

    Know where everyone will go. Check that it is accessible and suitable for the people in your household.

  3. 03

    Stay informed

    Know where to find official updates and be ready to follow local emergency advice.

Make the plan work for everyone.

  • Ask older relatives, young children, pregnant people and people with disabilities what support they need.
  • Agree who can help with transport, medicines and essential equipment.
  • Check on neighbours who may be isolated. Share official updates, not rumours.
  • Plan how children and animals will stay with the household when moving.
  • Bring boats in and secure them early, and agree where livestock will go.

If the route or safe place changes, use the latest local directions.
